Videos:
- YouTube videos no longer stop to rebuffer for half a minute a few minutes in. They are now fetched in pieces
- Swiping away from a video and back resumes it where you were, without loading it again (the last two are kept)
- Preloaded videos start with their sound in step with the picture, and a video whose sound drifts is put back in step

Feed:
- Clearing a filter or a search puts the Live Feed back where you were
- Opening a filter no longer marks its first slides as seen, so Audio and Articles keep loading more
- Videos, Audio, Images and Articles skip what you've seen, and fetch more of their type from your interests when they run low
- Flickr photos come from your interests, and photos tagged as adult are left out
- Flickr photos show as images, Reddit posts come once, and an article with no text shows its page's description
- Liked shows what you liked, not everything you saved
- A line under the top bar says when a source isn't responding, and Manage Sources marks it
- Each slide's source badge opens the original page, and openly licensed images show their licence
- Article titles and text no longer run behind the like, share and comment buttons

Sharing:
- Share on a web slide can now send its link to any app, with a short note about NoSlop, as well as share it to the mesh

Messages:
- Messages are hidden from screenshots, screen recordings and the recent-apps preview. You can turn this off in Settings

Notifications:
- New Phone notifications switch (on by default) at the top of Settings > Notifications. On Android 13 and newer NoSlop now asks for permission to show notifications

Layout:
- The HUBs tab is gone from this build, and the notifications icon is in the bottom menu on every page

Privacy:
- NoSlop now tells the sites it reads that it is NoSlop, with a contact address, rather than posing as a web browser. It still uses a browser's name where a site refuses NoSlop, for YouTube and Reddit, and for video playback and embedded players
- Official builds use no search proxy. Settings lets you add one you run yourself
- Report an Issue now says that reports become public GitHub issues
- The privacy policy says which server is involved (the bug-report relay) and what it sees, and what is and isn't encrypted on your phone

Languages:
- Every screen is now in your language. About 230 lines that still showed in English are translated into all 21 languages

Once downloaded, open the file from your Files app to install the update.
